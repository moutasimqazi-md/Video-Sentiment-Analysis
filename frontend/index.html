FAQ

Good questions

Can NeuroLens see my child's Instagram account?

No. Instagram doesn't share watch history, so NeuroLens only analyzes the Reels you give it, either as a link or a screen recording.

Is my video stored?

No. Uploaded and downloaded videos are deleted as soon as the analysis finishes. Only the scores are kept.

How accurate is it?

Treat it as a guide, not a verdict. Emotion is subjective, so every report shows the full breakdown, and you can mark a result as spot-on or off to help us improve.

Does the wellbeing signal detect dangerous content?

It reads emotional tone only. It can't detect explicit, violent or self-harm content, captions or comments, and it isn't a substitute for talking with your child or seeking professional help.

What files and links work?

Public Instagram Reel links, or MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV and WEBM files up to 300 MB.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Switch back to the free plan from your account page whenever you like.
