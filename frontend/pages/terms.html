What NeuroLens is

NeuroLens estimates the emotional tone of short videos from their visuals, soundtrack and pacing. The results are estimates meant to inform and start conversations. They are not facts, a diagnosis, or professional advice.

Using it responsibly

  • Only analyze videos you made, have permission to use, or that are publicly shared.
  • Don't upload content that is illegal or that you have no right to process.
  • Don't try to overload, probe or abuse the service.

Family monitoring

The wellbeing signal reflects emotional tone only. It cannot detect explicit, violent or self-harm content, captions or comments, and it will miss things. It is not a safety system. If you are worried about a child's safety, talk to them directly and contact a counselor, doctor or local crisis service. Parents and guardians are responsible for using the feature lawfully and with care for the child's privacy and trust.
