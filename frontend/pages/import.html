Import your Instagram & YouTube history

Download your history from each service, drop the files here, and get a habits report: when you scroll, how much, what it's about, and what to watch out for.

Your files stay on your device

  • Everything is read and analyzed in this browser. Your export is never uploaded.
  • Only history files are opened (watched, liked, saved, searches). Private messages and personal details are skipped and never read.
  • We keep a summary and a list of items for browsing, in this browser only. You can delete it any time.

Instagram

  1. Open Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Choose your account, then Some of your information. Tick Likes, Saved, Comments, Story interactions, Posts and Videos viewed (Ads information), Followers and following, Searches.
  3. Set the date range to Last year or All time, the format to JSON, and media quality to the lowest (media isn't needed).
  4. Wait for Instagram's email, download the .zip, and drop it below.

Instagram only keeps about the last 7 days of viewed reels in an export. Likes can go back a year or more, so long-term patterns come from those.

Open Instagram's export page (opens in a new tab)

YouTube (Google Takeout)

  1. Go to takeout.google.com and click Deselect all.
  2. Tick YouTube and YouTube Music, then All YouTube data included and keep history (add subscriptions and playlists if you like).
  3. Under Multiple formats, change history from HTML to JSON. This matters: HTML dates depend on your language.
  4. Create the export, download the .zip, and drop it below. Both services can be added together or one at a time.

YouTube history lists titles and channels but not watch time, so the report counts videos instead of minutes.

Open Google Takeout (opens in a new tab)
