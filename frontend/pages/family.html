Check the emotional tone of the Reels your child watches and get a heads-up when something looks heavy. It works best as a conversation starter, so we recommend being open with your child about it.

How to check what they watch

  • 1Open the Reel on their phone → Share → Copy link, then paste it under their profile.
  • 2For private accounts, screen-record the Reel and upload the recording.
  • 3Open the weekly report to see patterns and what to talk about.

NeuroLens can't read their Instagram account or watch history on its own. It analyzes the Reels you give it.
