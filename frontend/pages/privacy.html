Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026. The short version: we analyze your video, keep the scores, and delete the video.

What happens to your video

When you upload a video or paste a Reel link, the video is processed on our server only for as long as the analysis takes. It is deleted automatically as soon as the analysis finishes, or if something goes wrong. We do not keep copies of your footage.

What we keep

  • Analysis scores. The emotion percentages, pacing and color measurements, and a small preview image, so we can improve the service.
  • Feedback you give. If you mark a result as accurate or not, or suggest a better emotion, we store that with the scores.
  • The link you pasted. We log the link and the video title that were analyzed.

What stays in your browser

Your profile, plan, usage, child profiles, history and dashboard data are stored in your own browser (local storage). Clearing your browser data removes them. Nothing in this list is sent to us.

Imported Instagram and YouTube history

If you import your Instagram "Download your information" export or a Google Takeout file, it is opened and analyzed entirely in your browser. The file itself is never uploaded. Only history files are read (watched, liked, saved, searches, subscriptions); messages and personal details are skipped and never opened.

We keep a summary and a list of your history items in this browser's storage so you can browse the report later. You can delete it at any time from the report page. The only time anything from an import reaches our server is the optional "check the videos themselves" step, which sends the links you choose to be analyzed, and those results are saved like any other analysis.

Children

Family monitoring is meant for parents and guardians who analyze Reels they have access to. We encourage you to be open with your child about it. Child profiles contain only a name or nickname and an age that you enter, and they stay in your browser.

What we do not do

  • We do not read anyone's Instagram account, messages, comments or watch history.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not use tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can delete any analysis or child profile from within the site, and clear everything by clearing your browser data. To ask about data we hold server-side, contact us through the address your account administrator provided.
